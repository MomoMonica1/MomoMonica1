## Featured projects

### Bayesian statistics · R & Stan

**[Dota 2: Matchmaking Fairness & Win Factors](https://github.com/MomoMonica1/dota2-bayesian-analysis)**  
A Bayesian study of player win rates and match outcomes, combining hierarchical beta-binomial models with individual- and team-level logistic regression.

- **Methods:** Bayesian inference, MCMC, prior sensitivity, exploratory data analysis.
- **Explore:** R analysis pipeline, Stan models, data requirements, validation tests, and the original presentation.
- **Context:** DATASCI 451 team project, Winter 2025.

### Scientific computing · MATLAB

**[Nonlinear Mixed-Type PDE Solver](https://github.com/MomoMonica1/nonlinear-mixed-type-pde)**  
A numerical investigation of a PDE with forward- and backward-parabolic behavior, using finite differences, sparse linear systems, and iterative linearization.

- **Methods:** Vanishing-viscosity regularization, Picard iteration, boundary conditions, convergence checks.
- **Explore:** MATLAB solver and demo, numerical-method notes, validation tests, and the original research poster.
- **Context:** University of Michigan Laboratory of Geometry, 2025.
